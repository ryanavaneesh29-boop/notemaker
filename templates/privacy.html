Privacy

Privacy Policy

Note Maker is designed to keep your data safe and private.

What we collect

We store your account email, username, notes, subjects, and mind maps so your work is saved between sessions.

Why we store it

Your data is used only to authenticate your account, save your notes, mind maps, and subject preferences, and support password reset requests.

Cookies and local storage

We use an essential session cookie to keep you logged in. We also use local storage only for interface state like the last page you visited.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all saved content directly from this page if you are signed in.

Tracking

Note Maker does not use analytics or third-party tracking scripts. Your usage is not monitored beyond the data you explicitly save.

Delete account

Deleting your account removes all associated notes, mind maps, subjects, and login information from this service.
